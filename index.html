Good to know

Questions, answered

Is the macOS app signed and safe to open?

Yes. Public builds are signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so they open with a normal double-click. WindowSnap still runs entirely on your machine.

Why does it ask for Accessibility permission?

macOS requires Accessibility access to move other apps' windows. Open the menu-bar icon, choose Set Up Accessibility, and follow the system prompt. Shortcuts will not fire until that is granted.

Will it close apps or lose unsaved work?

No. WindowSnap repositions windows. It does not quit apps, force-close documents, or touch unsaved work.

How do I change the shortcuts?

Open the menu-bar icon, then Settings. Pick a binding and press the keys you want. Choose something deliberate so you never fire a snap by accident.

Is it really free? What is the catch?

No catch. It is free, with no account and no ads. The source is on GitHub if you want to read it or build it yourself.

What data does the app collect?

None. Clipboard history, snippets, and window layouts stay on your Mac. There is no telemetry, no account, and nothing leaves the machine.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu bar, then drag WindowSnap.app to the Trash. It leaves nothing lingering behind.
